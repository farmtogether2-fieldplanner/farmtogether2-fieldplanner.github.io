# Farm Planner Pro - Field Layout Tool for Farm Together 2
[Launch Project](farmtogether2-fieldplanner.github.io)
## Overview

Farm Planner Pro is a specialized web-based tool designed for Farm Together 2 players to plan and organize their farm layouts. This interactive planner allows you to create detailed farm designs before implementing them in-game, helping you optimize space usage and visualize your farm's appearance.

## Features

- **Custom Field Dimensions**: Create fields of any size up to 300×300 tiles (perfect for those large in-game farm plots)
- **Unlimited Products**: Add all your favorite Farm Together 2 crops, trees, and structures with custom colors
- **Powerful Planning Tools**:
  - Paint: Quickly fill areas with selected products
  - Erase: Remove products from your plan
  - Select: Choose multiple tiles for bulk operations
  - Lock: Prevent accidental changes to completed sections
- **Zoom Controls**: Easily navigate large farm layouts with zoom in/out functionality
- **Save & Load**: Store multiple farm designs and reload them later
- **Import/Export**: Share your farm designs with other players
- **Auto-Save**: Never lose your work with automatic saving every 2 minutes

## Getting Started

1. **Set Your Field Size**: Enter the width and height of your farm area (in tiles)
2. **Start Planning**: Use the color-coded products to place crops, buildings, and decorations
3. **Organize with Layers**: Lock completed sections to avoid accidental changes
4. **Save Your Design**: Give your plan a name and save it for future reference

## Tips for Farm Together 2 Players

- **Plan for Growth**: Design your farm with expansion in mind
- **Group Similar Items**: Place similar crops together for easier harvesting
- **Create Pathways**: Design clear paths between different farm areas for better navigation
- **Consider Irrigation**: Leave space for water sources near crop fields
- **Decoration Zones**: Allocate specific areas for decorative elements
- **Building Placement**: Position buildings strategically to minimize travel time

## Saving and Sharing

- All plans are saved locally in your browser
- Export your plans to share with friends or back them up
- Import plans created by other players for inspiration

## System Requirements

- Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- No installation required
- Works offline after initial page load
